The rules
Amber is not the action.

Brand energy, status, the hero accent word, the big figure. It never fills a button — amber on a CTA reads as a warning light.

Green means go — the CTA only.

--cta fills the one pill per view. Status green (--go) may outline a "Shipped" tag; it never fills anything.

Red is abort.

Errors, failed runs, destructive confirmations. Never emphasis, never a highlight.
