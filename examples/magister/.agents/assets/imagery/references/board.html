Magister — what the imagery should feel like

Based on the strategy — command, precision, confidence; the Apollo deck, the OP-1, the launch arc — here are images found on the web that have the qualities we're after. Each is captioned with the one thing it's here for. The last in each row is close but wrong. Picks are marked In.

Photography

The style, in plain words: the room where the operation runs, and the hands that run it. Consoles, screens, rows of operators seen from behind, a key under a thumb — never a face to camera. Low warm light from screens in a dark room; deep blacks; one highlight. Shot like a documentary still from the back of the room. Everything goes through one amber grade, so the archive and today sit in one world.

Illustration

The style, in plain words: flight plans. Technical line diagrams of the work — the trajectory from brief to shipped, the gate where you approve, the orbit of tools. Thin bone lines on the dark ground, one amber line for what's happening now, dotted for what's planned. No people, no 3D, no characters.
